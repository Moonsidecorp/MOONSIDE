CORE PHILOSOPHY

Plan. Execute. Review. Improve.

A trade isn't one decision — it's a process with four stages. Moonside is built around all four, not just the entry.

01
PLAN
Define the setup before entering — entry area, stop loss, and target, written down before emotion gets involved.
02
EXECUTE
Follow the predefined entry, stop loss, and target. Execution is where discipline is tested.
03
REVIEW
Measure what actually happened against what was planned — not what it felt like in the moment.
04
IMPROVE
Identify patterns across trades and continuously refine the process, setup by setup.
